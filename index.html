      📜 Official Rules & Osqvik Punishment

      How to Play Lades

      • The Goal: Catch your teammates slipping by getting them to accept an object from you without remembering the game — every Lades earns you +1 and gives them -1. Stack points, stay sharp, and make sure you're not in last place by December 5th to stay out of the freezing lake at Osqvik!
      • Voluntary Two-Hand Handoff: When handing something to a teammate, both hands must touch the object at the same time and the exchange must be voluntary by both parties — no throwing objects and no hiding the object in your hand.
      • Defend Yourself: Anytime you accept an object from a teammate, say "Aklımda!" or "On my mind!", or make it clearly obvious with a gesture (like tapping your temple) that you have it on your mind.
      • Timing & No Cooldown: There is no cooldown between Lades, so stay alert at all times. Call "LADES!" within about 5 seconds of handing over the object and log it above (+1 / -1). If the receiver remembers and says "Aklımda!" / "On my mind!" (or gestures) before you say "LADES!", no score is awarded.
      ⏸️ Official Game Pauses (Safe Zones)
      • Wednesday & Thursday Practices: The game pauses between the opening "KTH on 3!" huddle and the final "KTH on 3!" huddle.
      • Weekend Games: The game pauses the moment we enter the game court and resumes on the final whistle.
      • Mixed Practices Still Count: The game stays LIVE during mixed practices — even a basketball handoff counts as a score!
      ⚖️ Rule Disputes & Support: Any uncertainties or disagreements? Contact Atahan for support or the final verdict.
      ❄️ The Final Punishment: Osqvik Freezing Lake

      At our Osqvik Christmas Party on December 5th, whoever sits at the bottom of the scoreboard with the worst overall score gets thrown into the freezing lake. Ties don't save you — everyone tied for last place goes in!
